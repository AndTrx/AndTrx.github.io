01 / RESEARCH

From galactic nuclei
to their cosmic environments.

The gas between
galaxies tells a story.

I combine optical and near-infrared spectroscopy with X-ray observations to investigate the exchange of matter and energy between supermassive black holes, their host galaxies and the surrounding medium.

01

Quasars, feeding & feedback

Accretion and outflows in active galactic nuclei, and their role in the evolution of galaxies across cosmic time.

AGN · MULTIPHASE OUTFLOWS · X-RAYS

02

The circumgalactic medium

Mapping extended emission around quasars to constrain the density, ionization and kinematics of gas in their halos.

LYMAN-α NEBULAE · IFU SPECTROSCOPY · CLOUDY

03

Massive nodes of the cosmic web

Galaxy assembly, black-hole growth and the emergence of hot gas in dense environments in the distant Universe.

PROTOCLUSTERS · MQN01 · GALAXY ASSEMBLY

OBSERVATIONAL TOOLBOX

VLT / MUSE · Keck / KCWI · Chandra · VLT / KMOS & SINFONI
